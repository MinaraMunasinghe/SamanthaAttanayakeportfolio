Hi, I'm Samantha

I am a professional Western Music educator, creative artist, and performer, currently serving as a Western Music Teacher at GEMS School in Dubai. Holding a Bachelor of Arts (Special) in Western Music from the University of the Visual & Performing Arts, Sri Lanka, my career spans years of artistic expression and musical instruction. I bring an international standard of teaching to my students, blending deep knowledge of Western music theory and practical technique with a passion for the arts. Whether you are an absolute beginner or preparing for advanced musical milestones, my goal is to nurture your creative talents and build lasting musical confidence.
